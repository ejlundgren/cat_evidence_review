In this page we will plot and analyze the citation network uncovered by following all claimant and non-claimant sources regarding the effects of cats on threatened wildlife.

Claimant sources are reviews that claimed that cats were a driver of endangerment and are treated separately than non-claimants, which included a systematic search in Web of Science along with opportunistic records found and a published systematic review (Wallach and Lundgren 2025).

This workflow is derived from a full R script in scripts/2_analysis/1 citation networks and bias.R that includes additional checks and tests and is fully interactive if you clone the repo.

Probability of being cited

Now, let’s evaluate the terminus of each citation chain from the core claimant sources (the large bright pink points). What is the probability that those citation chains end in population studies?

We’ll load a separate data frame produced by scripts/1_data_mgmt/3 create probability of being cited dataset.R. This dataset is organized as claimant-claimant-source article pairs. Each row is a potential citation. The colum cited (0 or 1) indicates whether the article was cited or not. Pairs are constrained by species and publication timing (e.g., articles published after claimant reviews were finished are excluded).

This dataset allows us to evaluate whether claimants cited available evidence.

# Probability of being cited
Now, let's evaluate the terminus of each citation chain from the core claimant sources (the large bright pink points). What is the probability that those citation chains end in **population studies**?

We'll load a separate data frame produced by `scripts/1_data_mgmt/3 create probability of being cited dataset.R`. This dataset is organized as claimant-claimant-source article pairs. Each row is a *potential* citation. The colum `cited` (0 or 1) indicates whether the article was cited or not. Pairs are constrained by species and publication timing (e.g., articles published after claimant reviews were finished are excluded).

This dataset allows us to evaluate whether claimants cited available evidence.
