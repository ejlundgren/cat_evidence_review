Now let’s look at the timeline of the last records of extinct populations attributed to cat predation and the estimated arrival times of cats at those locations. These are reported based on the following categories based on population’s estimated extinction year and estimated cat arrival year:

  • Extinction before the arrival of cats
  • Extinction overlaps arrival of cats
  • Extinction within 0-100 years of cat arrival
  • Extinction 101-200 years after cat arrival
  • Extinction >200 years after cat arrival

This workflow corresponds to the full script at scripts/2_analysis/5 timeline analysis.R. Let’s start by cleaning the environment and loading our data:
